产品确认Product“退款按实付金额，优惠券不退，全额、部分退款都一样”“Refund the amount paid, coupons stay used, full or partial”

开发补充Engineering“支付平台可能重复回调，得保证同一笔退款只成功处理一次”“The payment platform can call back twice, each refund must succeed only once”

讨论澄清Clarified“别影响正常退款重试，失败后再重试成功，也要覆盖”“Don’t block normal retries, a success after a failure must still count”
